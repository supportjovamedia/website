# Selected visual direction

The user selected reference 8, Alignly by Iko Setiawan / One Week Wonders: https://dribbble.com/shots/25259523-Alignly-HR-Management-Dashboard. This is an independent JovaBook adaptation of the visual language, with no copied photos, logos or third-party assets.

Palette: white #ffffff, mist #f2f6f5, green #8bcf97, blue #40758a and ink #202622. Outfit is self-hosted with its OFL licence. Large numbers use tabular figures. Navigation and primary actions use green pills with dark text, with blue reserved for revenue and the next appointment. Containers are rounded, without decorative gradients or shadows.

Dashboard retains four statistics, Day/Week/Month controls and appointments. Calendar retains its mini calendar, next appointment and Filters beside the diary. Staff and management retain their table layouts. Public booking and authentication pages are outside this owner workspace theme. Existing saved workspace colour preferences are retained; Fresh green is added as a new default and selectable option.

Filled dots show one confirmed/completed booking each in the selected range and staff filter, capped at 40 with an explicit caption. Empty dots do not represent capacity. No sample data is added to app source, no backend schema changes are needed, and no new service is connected. Preview screenshots use isolated local demo records.

## Validation

Existing JavaScript syntax, 55 owner logic checks, Dashboard/Calendar date ranges and staff filters, appointment actions and desktop/tablet/mobile layouts. Screenshots and local preview are provided for visual review before any production merge.
