# JovaBook product page PRD

Updated 2 November 2026. This supersedes earlier visual proposals.

## Purpose

Help UK service-business owners understand the product, inspect its actual interface, choose a plan and start a workspace trial. Explain the offer clearly with real product evidence. No invented outcomes, rankings, customer endorsements or unavailable capabilities.

## Final page flow

1. White JovaMedia hero, business booking headline, real laptop/phone panel images and a free-trial CTA to plan selection.
2. Full original 1:42 film with controls and original audio. Clearly identify text reminders and slot recovery as team-plan previews.
3. Compact booking-system introduction with plan CTA. No decorative partner cards.
4. Isolated interactive booking walkthrough through Review only. Back left, Continue right. No editable personal details, database, messages, payment or confirmation action.
5. Real calendar presentation in a distinct grey section.
6. Complete, uncropped included business website with useful accompanying copy.
7. Website personalisation and six interactive real workspace themes.
8. Insights after website and workspace presentation.
9. Six photo-and-copy industry cards: hair and beauty, healthcare, education, coaches and trainers, consultation, spa and wellness.
10. Slim reference-led pricing columns in their own grey section. Standard is highlighted as most popular.
11. Exclusive FAQ, then JovaMedia brand-blue final CTA with no white seam.

Do not restore the rejected services/clients/team rail, setup carousel, scroll stoppers, problem-card grid, random partner artwork, red-dot labels, gradients or tiny footer-style pricing disclaimers.

## Commercial rules

- Monthly subscriptions: Essential GBP20, Standard GBP50, Premium GBP100.
- Annual subscriptions paid upfront: GBP192, GBP480, GBP960. Twenty percent off the subscription, commission billed monthly.
- Commission: Essential and Standard 1% of full service price when the appointment takes place; Premium 0%.
- Essential: one owner, no Staff or Insights, online booking, clients, services, calendar, one-page website and email reminders.
- Standard supports five staff and Insights. Premium supports ten staff, advanced Insights, a multi-page website and removal of platform branding. Contact the team for these plans during the Essential self-service launch.
- Primary CTAs navigate to plans. Essential selection carries chosen monthly/annual cadence to signup. Existing-account login stays available.
- The three-day workspace trial begins on registration and does not automatically create a paid subscription.
- Do not advertise live deposits until the separately gated Connect release is complete. Native Instagram booking is not available; sharing a booking link is supported.

## Visual and interaction acceptance

- Real full-HD originals remain accessible in keyboard-operable image dialogs. Responsive previews request enough source pixels for their rendered width.
- Alternating white/neutral-grey sections, readable typography, brand blue #235789 and restrained yellow accents.
- Non-blocking scroll reveals. No pinned sections, huge autoplay films or pause-animation controls.
- Exclusive accessible FAQ. Dialog keyboard focus and closing work. Theme buttons visibly update actual dashboard images.
- Desktop, phone and narrow checkout actions fit without page overflow. Mobile industry content uses cards.

## Search and measurement

Use one descriptive H1, meaningful feature headings, natural scheduling-software/service-business copy, accessible image alternatives, canonical metadata, SoftwareApplication/Product/VideoObject and visible FAQ structured data. Schema must match the actual offer. Preserve the canonical sitemap entry. After deployment, verify reachability/indexability and use existing free Search Console/Bing tools. Submission is not indexing or a ranking guarantee.
