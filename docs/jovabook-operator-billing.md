# Operator account and recorded billing contract

All operator RPCs recheck the selected verified operator using the private allowlist. Auth email, not editable user metadata, supplies `account_email`. `business_email` is included only when the business contact differs. No bank/card details, customer lists, credentials or provider identifiers are returned.

`operator_overview()` keeps `total`, recent signups, month history, sign-in and notification fields. Essential, Standard and Premium counts now include only `ever_paid` accounts, including historical paid accounts that are currently past due or cancelled. `paid_total` is their total. `paid_active` separately counts verified paid coverage with no recorded failure. `trials_active`, `trials_ended` and `trials_total` include only unpaid accounts that actually started a trial. A repeat registration without a trial is not counted as a new trial.

`operator_businesses(p_search)` searches name, slug, authoritative Auth email and business contact email. Each record keeps its existing fields and adds `account_email`, nullable `business_email`, `has_paid`, `billing_state`, `commission` and `estimate`. Billing states are `trial`, `trial_ended`, `payment_required`, `paid`, `past_due` and `cancelled`. A scheduled cancellation retains paid coverage and exposes its existing `cancel_at_period_end` flag. `operator_business(p_id)` includes the same fields and a complete `billing_summary`.

`operator_billing_summary(p_id)` returns `{ok,currency,subscription,commission,estimate,batches}`. All amounts are integer GBP pence. The subscription projection contains recorded plan, cadence, paid coverage, provider status, failure/verification timestamps, cancellation and pending change fields. These are recorded entitlement facts, not an exact history of cash received.

Commission fields are `settled_minor`, `settled_gross_minor`, `issued_credits_minor`, `invoiced_outstanding_minor`, `unbilled_minor`, `unresolved_count`, `review_required`, `available`, `tracking_enabled`, `recorded`, `updated_at`, `period_start` and `period_end`. Settled and outstanding amounts subtract issued credits from the relevant settled or invoiced batches. Open entries alone form unbilled commission; allocated and reserved entries are excluded. Unknown history, pending credit review and unresolved invoice cycles are not guessed. Unbilled amounts are null when unresolved; accounts with no commission records have null financial totals. Aggregate overview totals also expose `businesses_available` and `businesses_unavailable` to identify incomplete coverage.

Use the label **Settled commission**, not cash collected. A settled batch can use verified payment, customer balance, credit waiver or mixed coverage. Exact cash allocation is not persisted: `cash_collected_minor` is null and `cash_collected_available` is false.

The estimate whitelist includes the recorded cycle/cadence, subscription due date, cached `updated_at`, `stale`, and existing `_pence` estimate fields. It is unavailable when absent, older than 24 hours, disabled, missing consent or blocked by unresolved work. Unavailable monetary estimates are null. Annual subscriptions remain prepaid; their commission cadence remains monthly. Dashboard reads do not refresh or recalculate statements.

The newest 20 batches expose opaque `reference` and `invoice_reference` digests, cutoff, commission amount, issued credits, `local_status`, `collection_state`, `settlement_kind` and `recorded_at`. The cutoff means **Billed through**, not a provider invoice due date. `recorded_at` is not a verified payment timestamp. Complete provider invoice statuses, due dates and historical subscription cash totals are not stored in this schema.

Only existing directory/detail audit writes remain. The private financial helpers and dedicated billing-summary RPC only select existing records. Trial history retention and fraud checks are unchanged. Full isolated SQL tests exercise paid/trial separation, contact provenance, credits/allocation, annual estimates, stale/unknown amounts, private projections, role denial and operator revocation.
