# Portfolio previews

The homepage and Work page show three PNG screenshots of Sumera’s, Qaiser Watches and Al Eiman. The previews have no website links or click actions. Each image can be scrolled inside its existing preview frame.

The six earlier concepts are saved in `lib/portfolio-concepts-archive.json`. Their original PNG assets remain in `public/home-selected/`, unchanged. To restore them later, use the archived entries in `components/SelectedConcepts.js`.

The new PNGs were captured from the actual websites at a 1440px desktop width. Next.js optimises their delivery for each screen size. Original captures and the export verification report are retained locally in `output/JovaMedia Portfolio/`.
