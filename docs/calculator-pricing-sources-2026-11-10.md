# Calculator pricing sources, checked 10 November 2026

The homepage comparison now offers Fresha, Treatwell, Booksy and Squarespace. Acuity was researched during this work and then removed from the public choices at Abdul’s request. The unused model and research below remain for recovery. No competitor website charge is included unless the visitor adds a real cost. These are illustrative comparisons before tax, not quotations or promises of savings. Payment processor charges and differences in features are outside the calculation.

## Acuity basis

- [Official pricing](https://www.acuityscheduling.com/pricing/): Starter is US$20 per month on monthly billing, with one calendar. The US$16 monthly figure requires annual billing and is not used. Applicable taxes are excluded. The page was opened and checked on 10 November 2026.
- [Official billing guide](https://help.acuityscheduling.com/hc/en-us/articles/47334812945805-Acuity-billing-if-you-use-Acuity-s-log-in-process): Acuity-managed billing uses USD; a financial institution can apply its own conversion. Squarespace-managed billing may offer other currencies. This implementation does not claim a published GBP Acuity price.
- [Payment fees](https://help.acuityscheduling.com/hc/en-us/articles/16676947528205-Accepting-payments): payment processors charge their own fees. Acuity generally adds no payment fee; a legacy subscription exception exists. No acquisition commission is assumed for the selected current Starter plan.
- ECB rates for 9 November 2026: [USD per EUR 1.1193](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/eurofxref-graph-usd.en.html), [GBP per EUR 0.83430](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/eurofxref-graph-gbp.en.html). Both date/rate pairs were checked on the official pages. Calculation: 20 × 0.83430 ÷ 1.1193 = 14.90753149, rounded to £14.91.
- This dated rate is fixed source metadata. The calculator makes no live FX requests. Card spreads, fees, tax and later currency moves can change an actual bill. Visitors can replace the estimate with their GBP bill.
- With no separately paid website added, £14.91 is below JovaBook Solo's £20. If the retained model is used, it shows JovaBook costing £5.09 more monthly (£61.08 more yearly), without a red rejection mark or a savings claim.

## Other retained inputs

[Booksy's UK page](https://biz.booksy.com/en-gb/pricing), opened on 10 November, still confirms £40 monthly plus £5 for each extra user, before VAT. Optional Boost is 30% on an eligible first visit, minimum £5. Boost stays off by default.

[Treatwell's UK page](https://www.treatwell.co.uk/partners/pricing/), opened on 10 November, still confirms Connect Freelancer £9.99, Advanced £24.99, and 35% on qualifying new marketplace bookings. Its 365-day new-client condition remains documented. Payment processing is separate.

Fresha and Squarespace GBP presets were retained from the existing 7 November source basis. Fresha's en-GB URL returned USD localization in this research environment, so those dollar amounts were not relabelled as GBP or used to alter the existing UK assumptions. This follow-up does not claim a fresh UK currency verification for those presets. Their official source links remain available in the calculator.

The five-client, £50 average first-visit assumption remains editable and clearly illustrative. It is not measured customer performance or guaranteed acquisition. Website and other optional charges start at £0 unless selected or entered. Different providers' benefits and allowances are not identical.
