# Device frame sources

MacBook Pro and iPhone X raster mockups from pixelsign/html5-device-mockups:
https://github.com/pixelsign/html5-device-mockups

Original files: device-mockups/MacbookPro/portrait_black.png and device-mockups/iPhoneX/portrait.png.
Retrieved 14 October 2026. The repository distributes these under the MIT licence, retained in LICENSE.txt. Original designer credits are retained in CREDITS.txt.

These are sourced device mockups, not generative images. The delivered WebP copies are compressed without retaining source metadata. Unmodified PNG originals are retained separately in the workspace output/device-mockups/originals folder.
