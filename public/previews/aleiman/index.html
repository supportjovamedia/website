Your packing checklist.

Keep the essentials together before you set off.

  • Passport, visa and booking documents
  • Ihram clothing and comfortable footwear
  • Personal toiletries and any prescribed medicines
  • Phone charger, travel adapter and power bank
  • A small bag for daily essentials
  • Copies of important contact details
