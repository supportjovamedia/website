THE MEANING OF UMRAH

A visit with
meaning.

Umrah is the pilgrimage to Makkah that can be undertaken throughout the year. The Arabic word means “to visit a populated place”. It is also known as the lesser pilgrimage.

The journey includes entering the sacred state of Ihram at the Miqat, performing Tawaf around the Kaaba and completing Sa’ee between Safa and Marwah, followed by shaving or trimming the hair.

BEFORE YOU TRAVEL

Prepare your body.
Prepare your mind.

Physical preparation

Umrah can involve considerable physical exertion. The original Al Eiman guide recommends preparing through exercise, including longer walks, so you feel ready for the walking involved.

Spiritual preparation

Take time to learn the rituals and reflect on the meaning behind them. Reading about other pilgrims’ experiences, watching documentaries and using a guidebook can help you visualise the journey ahead.

01

Entering Ihram

YOUR INTENTION AND SACRED STATE
  1. Prepare by removing unwanted hair, clipping your nails and trimming your moustache where applicable.
  2. Make Ghusl if possible, or perform Wudhu. Wear the clothing of Ihram before arriving at the Miqat.
  3. You may pray two raka’at. Make your intention to perform Umrah.
  4. Enter the state of Ihram and recite the Talbiyah. Continue towards Makkah and Al Masjid al Haram, repeating the Talbiyah.
THE INTENTION

“O Allah, I intend to perform Umrah; make it easy for me and accept from me.”

THE TALBIYAH

Labbayka Allaahumma labbayk, labbayka laa shareeka laka labbayk, innal-hamda wan-ni’mata laka wal-mulk, laa shareeka lak.

“Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. All praise and blessings, and all sovereignty are Yours. You have no partner.”

02

Performing Tawaf

SEVEN CIRCUITS AROUND THE KAABA
  1. Before beginning, men uncover the right shoulder. This is known as Idtiba.
  2. At Al Hajar al Aswad, raise your right hand to make Istilaam, saying “Bismillahi, Allahu Akbar”.
  3. Begin walking anticlockwise, keeping the Kaaba on your left. Complete seven circuits.
  4. Where possible, men walk briskly for the first three circuits, known as Raml, and walk at a normal pace for the remaining four.
  5. You may recite Quran, make Dua and remember Allah throughout Tawaf. Between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone, recite the supplication below.
  6. After Tawaf, cover the right shoulder and pray two raka’at behind Maqam Ibrahim. If there is no space, another place in the mosque will suffice.
  7. Drink Zamzam water after prayer, remembering the etiquettes and supplications of the Prophet, peace be upon him.
BETWEEN THE YEMENI CORNER AND THE BLACK STONE

Rabbanaa aatina fid-dunyaa hasanah wa fil aakhirati hasanah wa qinaa ‘adhaaban naar.

“Our Lord, grant us goodness in this world and goodness in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.”

03

Completing Sa’ee

BETWEEN SAFA AND MARWAH
  1. Proceed to Safa. Ascend the raised area, face the direction of the Kaaba and make supplication.
  2. Walk towards Marwah, reciting Quran, making Dua and remembering Allah along the way.
  3. Men walk briskly or jog between the two points marked by green lights.
  4. At Marwah, face the direction of the Kaaba and make Dua again. Your journey from Safa to Marwah counts as one circuit.
  5. Complete seven circuits in total, finishing at Marwah on the seventh.
04

Shaving or trimming

COMPLETING YOUR UMRAH
  1. After Sa’ee, men shave or trim their hair. Barber shops are available for this.
  2. Women gather their hair and cut approximately a fingertip’s length from the end.

Your Umrah is now complete and the restrictions of Ihram are lifted. May Allah accept your pilgrimage.

TIME TO REFLECT

Your supplications.

Make Dua with sincerity and purity of heart. These supplications are included in Al Eiman’s original guide, with their meanings to help you reflect.

For guidance, protection and blessings

اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِنِي فِيمَنْ هَدَيْتَ ، وَعَافِنِي فِيمَنْ عَافَيْتَ ، وَتَوَلَّنِي فِيمَنْ تَوَلَّيْتَ ، وَبَارِكْ لِي فِيمَا أَعْطَيْتَ ، وَقِنِي شَرَّ مَا قَضَيْتَ ، إِنَّكَ تَقْضِي وَلَا يُقْضَى عَلَيْكَ ، إِنَّهُ لَا يَذِلُّ مَنْ وَالَيْتَ ، وَلَا يَعِزُّ مَنْ عَادَيْتَ ، تَبَارَكْتَ رَبَّنَا وَتَعَالَيْتَ

MEANINGO Allah, guide me among those You have guided, grant me well-being among those You have granted well-being, take me into Your care and bless what You have given me. Protect me from the evil You have decreed. You decree, and none decrees over You. Those in Your care are not humiliated. Blessed and Exalted are You, our Lord.

TRANSLITERATIONAllaahumma-hdinee fee man hadayt, wa ‘aafinee fee man ‘aafayt, wa tawallanee fee man tawallayt, wa baarik lee fee maa a‘tayt, wa qinee sharra maa qadayt, fa innaka taqdee wa laa yuqdaa ‘alayk, innahu laa yadhillu man waalayt, wa laa ya‘izzu man ‘aadayt, tabaarakta rabbanaa wa ta‘aalayt.

For acceptance of prostration

اللَّهُمَّ اكْتُبْ لِي بِهَا عِنْدَكَ أَجْرًا ، وَضَعْ عَنِّي بِهَا وِزْرًا ، وَاجْعَلْهَا لِي عِنْدَكَ ذُخْرًا ، وَتَقَبَّلْهَا مِنِّي كَمَا تَقَبَّلْتَهَا مِنْ عَبْدِكَ دَاوُدَ

MEANINGO Allah, record a reward for me for this prostration and remove a sin from me. Store it for me and accept it from me as You accepted it from Your servant Dawood, peace be upon him.

TRANSLITERATIONAllaahumm-aktub lee bihaa ‘indaka ajran, wa da‘ ‘annee bihaa wizran, waj‘alhaa lee ‘indaka dhukhran, wa taqabbalhaa minnee kamaa taqabbaltahaa min ‘abdika daawud.

For forgiveness, mercy and sustenance

لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ ، وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ ، اللهُ أَكْبَرُ كَبِيرًا ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلهِ كَثِيرًا ، سُبْحَانَ اللهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللهِ الْعَزِيزِ الْحَكِيمِ ، اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي ، وَارْحَمْنِي ، وَاهْدِنِي ، وَارْزُقْنِي

MEANINGNone has the right to be worshipped except Allah alone, without partner. Allah is most great, and much praise is for Allah. Glory be to Allah, Lord of the worlds. There is no might or power except with Allah, the Mighty, the Wise. O Allah, forgive me, have mercy on me, guide me and grant me sustenance.

TRANSLITERATIONLaa ilaaha ill-Allaahu wahdahu laa shareeka lah, Allaahu akbaru kabeeran, wal-hamdu lillaahi katheeran, subhaan-Allaahi rabbil-‘aalameen, laa hawla wa laa quwwata illaa billaahil-‘azeezil-hakeem. Allaahumm-aghfir lee, warhamnee, wahdinee, warzuqnee.
